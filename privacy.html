Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Product Owner: Hasnain Ali

Summary: You own 100% of your audio, transcripts, summaries, and meeting intelligence. We never sell your personal data, nor do we train public generative foundation models on your confidential conversations.

1. Overview & Data Architecture

MeetMind AI ("the Platform", "we", or "our") provides intelligent meeting capture, transcription, executive synthesis, and task management. Our storage and processing architecture comprises:

  • Client-Side Local Storage: Temporary browser cache and offline storage for instant low-latency application interaction.
  • Relational Database Layer: Secure SQLite / PostgreSQL backend managing user profiles, workspace permissions, structured summaries, and task audit trails.
  • Optional Cloud Synchronization: When authenticated via Google Firebase, meeting metadata and user preferences synchronize across your authorized devices using Google Cloud Firestore.

2. Information We Collect & Process

  • Account Identity: Display name, verified email address, organization name, and optional avatar URL collected during authentication.
  • Meeting Audio & File Uploads: Audio streams recorded via browser microphone or uploaded audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A) submitted strictly for speech-to-text inference.
  • Extracted Meeting Intelligence: Diarized text transcripts, executive briefs, decisions, deadlines, and action items generated by our AI engine.
  • Third-Party Integration Tokens: Encrypted OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens (secured with AES-256-GCM) when you choose to connect Google Calendar, Slack, Jira, Trello, or Notion.

3. AI Service Providers & Inference Processing

To deliver rapid, bilingual English and Urdu transcription and multi-meeting reasoning, MeetMind AI utilizes enterprise-grade AI infrastructure:

  • Google Gemini API (Gemini 2.5 Flash): Utilized for structured executive summaries, risk identification, semantic comparison, and universal meeting chat. Data transmitted via API is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and is governed by enterprise terms that prohibit data usage for public model training.
  • Speech Recognition Engine: Browser Web Speech API for real-time live transcription, and server-side Whisper / Ollama Cloud inference for uploaded audio files.

4. Data Retention, Backups & Deletion Rules

We believe in strict data minimization and user sovereignty:

  • Active Data: Retained in your account until you initiate deletion or terminate your workspace.
  • User-Initiated Deletion: Deleting a meeting from the Library permanently purges its record, diarized chunks, and embeddings from the active database immediately.
  • Backup & Log Retention: Transient server operational logs and automated database snapshots cycle out and are permanently overwritten on a rolling schedule within a maximum of 30 calendar days.

5. Recording Consent & Legal Compliance

Important User Obligation: You acknowledge and agree that you are solely responsible for complying with all applicable local, provincial, and international recording and wiretapping laws. You must notify all meeting participants and obtain all necessary consents before recording any audio.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or meeting transcripts to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service (cloud hosting, database persistence, and AI inference APIs).

7. Data Export & Account Closure

You may export your complete meeting library at any time in standardized JSON or Markdown format from the Settings & Vault tab. You may request full account termination and data purge by contacting our privacy desk.
