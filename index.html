FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have clear answers.

Does MeetMind AI support Urdu and Pakistani English accents?

Yes! MeetMind AI is specifically engineered for bilingual Pakistani teams. You can switch between pure English, Urdu (اردو), or Bilingual code-switching mode to transcribe natural everyday conversations accurately.

What audio and document formats can I upload?

You can upload audio in MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, and WebM formats up to 500 MB. For documents, PDF, DOCX, and plain text notes are supported.

How is my meeting data handled and protected?

Your audio and transcripts belong exclusively to you. You can export all your meeting summaries in JSON/Markdown at any time, or permanently delete any recording and transcript with one click from the library.

Can I install MeetMind AI on my iPhone or Android?

Yes. MeetMind AI is a modern Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your Android or iPhone home screen with one tap—no large 100MB download from the app store needed.
