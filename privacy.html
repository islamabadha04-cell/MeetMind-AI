Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026 • Product Owner: Hasnain Ali

1. Overview & Commitment

MeetMind AI ("we", "our", or "the Service") operates the meeting assistant platform accessible at meetmind-ai-74519.web.app. We respect your confidentiality and ensure your audio recordings, transcripts, and action items belong entirely to you.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When signing in with Google or email, we receive your email address and display name.
  • Meeting Audio & Notes: Audio captured via microphone or file upload is processed strictly to generate transcription, executive summaries, and action items.
  • Usage & Telemetry: Anonymized interaction telemetry to improve processing speed and speech recognition accuracy.

3. How Your Data Is Handled

Your meeting data is processed using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and state-of-the-art neural speech models. We do not sell your personal data or meeting transcripts to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You have full sovereignty over your data. You may export all your meetings in JSON/Markdown at any time from Settings, or permanently delete individual meetings from the Library. Deleting a meeting removes its transcript and summary from cloud storage immediately.
