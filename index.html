FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your operational and security inquiries.

Does MeetMind AI support Urdu and Pakistani English accents?

Yes. MeetMind AI is specifically engineered for bilingual Pakistani teams. You can switch between pure English, Urdu (اردو), or bilingual code-switching mode to transcribe natural everyday conversations with zero translation distortion.

What audio and document formats can I upload?

You can upload audio in MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, and WebM formats directly from your device. For notes, plain text (.txt, .md, .json) documents are fully supported.

How is my meeting data handled and protected?

Your audio and transcripts belong exclusively to you. You can export all your meeting summaries in JSON or Markdown at any time, or permanently purge any recording from your personal library with one click.

Can I install MeetMind AI on my iPhone or Android?

Yes. MeetMind AI is a modern Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your Android or iPhone home screen with one tap without needing to download a large 100MB file from an app store.
